Migraine diary apps for Android, compared on facts

We make Migrely, so we are not neutral. This page sticks to what each app's own Google Play listing, permission list, Data safety section, website and privacy policy said on 29 October 2026, with links, and it lists what each app does well. Prices and features change, so check the sources before you choose.

Which apps have no internet permission?

Two of these diaries request no internet permission on Android: Migrely and the open-source Migraine Log. The others request "full network access", according to their Google Play permission lists. Nagi and Dark Logger say their diaries work offline; their privacy policies list what they use the network for, such as weather.

Which apps need an account?

Bearable and Migraine Monitor require one. Migraine Buddy's help center says your data synchronizes with its server and is linked with your account. Nagi, Dark Logger and Migrely need none, and Migraine Log mentions none.
