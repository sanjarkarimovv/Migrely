Questions

Which phones does Migrely work on?

Android phones with Android 8 or later and Google Play. There is no iPhone version.

Is there an account?

No. There is no account to make and nothing to sign in to.

Where are the entries kept?

On the phone. Migrely has no internet permission, so Android stops it from connecting. Android's own backup may copy entries to a Google account. On Android 9 and later Migrely allows this only end-to-end encrypted, which needs a screen lock; without one, the entries move only by direct transfer to a new phone. On Android 8 they are backed up like other apps' data. Exported files go wherever they are sent.

What if Google Play doesn't open?

The buttons open Migrely's listing on Google Play. If it doesn't load, Google Play may be blocked on this network or device, or Migrely may not be offered in this country. Search for “Migrely” in the Google Play app, or write to support@migrely.health.
