Keeping a migraine log for a VA claim

Migrely is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA). This page is general information, not legal or medical advice, and no log or app can promise a rating. Accredited Veterans Service Organization (VSO) representatives help with VA claims for free.

How does VA rate migraine?

VA rates migraine under diagnostic code 8100 by how often you have "prostrating" attacks, on average, over the last several months:

38 CFR 4.124a, diagnostic code 8100, read on 29 October 2026
RatingVA's words
50%With very frequent completely prostrating and prolonged attacks productive of severe economic inadaptability
30%With characteristic prostrating attacks occurring on an average once a month over last several months
10%With characteristic prostrating attacks averaging one in 2 months over last several months
0%With less frequent attacks

VA can change its rating schedule. The current text is on eCFR.

What does "prostrating" mean?

VA's headaches questionnaire for examiners (the DBQ) says: "For VA purposes, the term prostrating means 'causing extreme exhaustion, powerlessness, debilitation or incapacitation with substantial inability to engage in ordinary activities.'" It then asks how often such attacks happened, on average, over the last several months.

What should a migraine log record?

  • the date of every attack, the mild ones too, so the record is complete;
  • when it started and when it ended;
  • how bad it got;
  • what it stopped you doing, such as lying down in a dark room or leaving work;
  • the medicines you took, and whether they helped;
  • work or plans you missed.

Keep it for months, written at the time or soon after. A steady, honest record is easier for your doctor, your representative and the examiner to rely on than memory.

Who can help with a claim?

VA accredits three kinds of representatives: VSO representatives, attorneys and claims agents. VA says the services an accredited VSO representative provides on your claim are always free. You can find a VA-accredited representative or VSO on VA's website.

When should I get help straight away?

Call your emergency number if a headache comes on suddenly and is extremely painful, or comes with weakness on one side, problems speaking or loss of vision. The NHS migraine page lists every warning sign.
