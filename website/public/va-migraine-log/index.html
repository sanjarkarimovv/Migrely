Keeping a migraine log for a VA claim

Migrely is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), and it is not a VA-accredited representative, so it cannot help you prepare or file a claim. This page is general information, not legal or medical advice, and no log or app can promise a rating. Accredited Veterans Service Organization (VSO) representatives help with VA claims for free.

How does VA rate migraine?

VA rates migraine under diagnostic code 8100. The 10% and 30% levels depend on how often "prostrating" attacks happen, on average, over the last several months. The 50% level also asks whether attacks are completely prostrating and prolonged and cause "severe economic inadaptability". In the regulation's words:

38 CFR 4.124a, diagnostic code 8100, read on 29 October 2026
RatingVA's words
50%With very frequent completely prostrating and prolonged attacks productive of severe economic inadaptability
30%With characteristic prostrating attacks occurring on an average once a month over last several months
10%With characteristic prostrating attacks averaging one in 2 months over last several months
0%With less frequent attacks

VA can change its rating schedule. The current text is on eCFR.

What does "prostrating" mean?

VA's headaches Disability Benefits Questionnaire (DBQ), the form doctors complete for VA, says: "For VA purposes, the term prostrating means 'causing extreme exhaustion, powerlessness, debilitation or incapacitation with substantial inability to engage in ordinary activities.'" It then asks how often such attacks happened, on average, over the last several months.

What should a migraine log record?

  • the date of every attack, the mild ones too, so the record is complete;
  • when it started and when it ended;
  • how bad it got;
  • what you could still do and what you had to stop, for example: carried on, slowed down, stopped, or lay down;
  • the medicines you took, and whether they helped;
  • work or plans you missed.

Keep it for months, written at the time or soon after. A steady, honest record is easier for your doctor and your representative to rely on than memory.

What does Migrely record?

Migrely, our app for Android, keeps the start and end of each attack, the worst pain, the medicines you took and a note. "Impact on your day" lets you record how each attack affected you: No impact, Less active, Had to stop or Bed rest. You choose these labels for your own record. They are Migrely's words, not VA's terms, and what counts as prostrating is for VA to decide.

Exporting a spreadsheet (Export for Excel, CSV) is free. It has one row per attack, with its start, end, duration, peak pain, symptoms, medicines, impact and note. The PDF report for appointments is part of Premium.

Who can help with a claim?

VA accredits three kinds of representatives: VSO representatives, attorneys and claims agents. VA says the services an accredited VSO representative provides on your claim are always free. Some accredited attorneys and claims agents also help for free on initial claims; VA lets them charge fees once it has decided your initial claim. You can find a VA-accredited representative or VSO on VA's website.

When should I get help straight away?

Call your emergency number if a headache comes on suddenly and is extremely painful, or comes with weakness on one side, problems speaking or loss of vision. The NHS migraine page lists every warning sign.

Veterans in crisis can dial 988, then press 1, or text 838255 (Veterans Crisis Line).
