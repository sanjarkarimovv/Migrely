Check Migrely's privacy yourself

Many apps promise privacy. With Migrely you can check it: the app has no internet permission, so Android itself stops it from connecting to the internet. It takes about two minutes.

Does Migrely connect to the internet?

No. An Android app can reach the internet only if it asks for the internet permission, and Migrely does not ask for it. Android then blocks every connection the app tries, so nothing you enter can leave your phone through Migrely.

How can I check it on my phone?

  1. Open your phone's Settings, then Apps, then Migrely.
  2. Tap Permissions.
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top, then All permissions.
  4. Look for “have full network access”. Apps that use the internet list it. Migrely does not.

The names can differ a little between phone makers. The check for developers below is the same on every phone.

Does airplane mode prove it?

It shows half of it. Turn on airplane mode and Migrely works as usual: logging, your history, reminders and export all work offline. That shows Migrely does not need the internet; the permission list shows it cannot use it. Buying or restoring Premium, and rating Migrely, need Google Play to be online.

What are Migrely's five permissions for?

These are all the permissions Migrely asks for. Those in quotes appear in the list under those names. On phones with Google Play, billing may be listed too.

“show notifications”

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Reminders and the “Did it help?” question. On Android 13 and later, Android asks you first. On any version you can turn notifications off in Settings.

“run at startup”

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Puts your reminders back after the phone restarts. It comes with WorkManager, the Android library Migrely uses to schedule reminders.

“prevent phone from sleeping”

android.permission.WAKE_LOCK

Lets WorkManager keep the phone awake for the moments it takes to run a reminder.

Google Play billing

com.android.vending.BILLING

Lets Migrely ask the Google Play app on your phone whether Premium is on, and open Google Play's purchase screen. Migrely talks only to the Google Play app, never to the internet; Google Play itself goes online to complete a purchase.

An internal AndroidX permission

com.migrely.app.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION

AndroidX, Google's standard library for Android apps, adds it so that on older Android versions only Migrely can send messages to its own parts. Settings does not list it.

What can still leave my phone?

Without the internet permission, Migrely itself sends nothing. Three things are up to Android, Google Play or you:

  • Android's own backup. Android may back up Migrely's entries to your Google account, as it does for other apps. On Android 9 and later Migrely allows this only end-to-end encrypted, which needs a screen lock; on Android 8 it is backed up like other apps' data. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.
  • Google Play. When you buy Premium or rate Migrely, Google Play's own window handles it, under Google's privacy policy. Migrely never sees your payment details or your rating.
  • Files you share. A backup, spreadsheet or PDF report goes wherever you choose to send it.

The privacy policy has the details.

How can a developer check it?

With USB debugging on and Migrely installed, run:

adb shell dumpsys package com.migrely.app | grep -A5 "requested permissions"

It prints the same five permissions. To look for the internet permission alone, run:

adb shell dumpsys package com.migrely.app | grep INTERNET

It prints nothing.
