Help

Answers to common questions. Anything else: write to support@migrely.health.

Does Migrely send my data anywhere?

No. Migrely has no internet access at all, so nothing you enter can leave your phone through the app. There is no account, no tracking and no ads.

Is Migrely free?

Logging, your whole history, medications, reminders, export and every safety warning are free. Premium adds the doctor report and Insights. The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial; there is also a monthly plan and a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I move my diary to a new phone?

Android's own backup brings it to a new phone signed in to the same Google account. You can also export a backup file in Settings, Your data, and import it on the new phone.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Premium belongs to your Google account. Install Migrely with the same account and it is found at the first start; Settings, Premium, Restore purchases checks again on demand.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Google Play: Profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Migrely Premium, Cancel. Cancel before a free trial ends and you are not charged. Your entries are never deleted.

How do I delete everything?

In Migrely: Settings, Delete all data. Type DELETE to confirm. Everything Migrely keeps on your phone is removed at once.

Is Migrely a medical device?

No. Migrely is a diary that helps you keep a record and talk to your doctor. It does not diagnose or treat. If a headache comes with any warning sign, such as the worst pain of your life or weakness that does not pass, call your local emergency number.

Migrely is a diary. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a doctor about your headaches.
