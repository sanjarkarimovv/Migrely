Migrely help

Answers about privacy, backups, Premium and cancelling. Questions asked before installing are on the home page. Anything else: write to support@migrely.health.

What can leave my phone?

Migrely itself sends nothing: it has no internet permission, so Android stops it from connecting. Three things are up to Android, Google Play or you. Android's own backup may copy Migrely's entries to your Google account. On Android 9 and later Migrely allows this only end-to-end encrypted, which needs a screen lock; without one, the entries move only by direct transfer to a new phone. On Android 8 they are backed up like other apps' data. Google Play handles buying Premium and ratings, under Google's privacy policy. And a backup, spreadsheet or PDF report goes wherever you choose to send it.

What does Premium cost?

Premium is $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, $4.99 a month, or $59.99 once (US prices). The yearly plan's free trial is for new subscribers. Google Play shows the price in your currency before you pay and handles the payment, so we never see your card. Premium adds the doctor report and Insights; everything else is free.

How do I move my diary to a new phone?

Use Android's own transfer or a backup file. When you set up the new phone from the old one, by cable or nearby transfer, Android brings Migrely's entries with it. It can also restore them from your Google account's backup, which on Android 9 and later exists only if the old phone had a screen lock. Otherwise, on the old phone open Settings, Your data, Export a backup (JSON), copy the file to the new phone, and choose Import a backup in the same place there.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Install Migrely with the Google account you bought Premium with, and it is found at the first start. If it is not, open Settings, Premium, Restore purchases, which checks Google Play again for what that account bought. Premium belongs to your Google account, not to the phone.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Google Play: Profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Migrely Premium, Cancel. Cancel before a free trial ends and you are not charged. After you cancel, Premium stays on until the trial or the period you paid for ends. Your entries are never deleted.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds follow Google Play's refund policies, because Google Play handles the payment, so ask Google Play for one. If Google Play refunds and removes the purchase, Premium turns off at Migrely's next check; your entries stay.

Google Play's refund policies

How do I delete everything?

In Migrely, open Settings, Delete all data, and type DELETE to confirm. Everything Migrely keeps on your phone is removed at once. Android's backup of it is replaced the next time your phone backs up, and deleting does not cancel a subscription.

Is Migrely a medical device?

No. Migrely is a diary that helps you keep a record and talk to your doctor. It does not diagnose or treat. If a headache comes with any of the emergency signs that our guides list, call your local emergency number.

When to get help straight away

Migrely is a diary. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a doctor about your headaches.
