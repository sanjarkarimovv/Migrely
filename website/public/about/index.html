About Migrely

Migrely is a calm, private migraine diary for Android. Here is who makes it, how it is paid for, and what it will never do.

Who makes Migrely?

Migrely is published on Google Play by Sulton UzDev. Questions and ideas reach us at support@migrely.health.

Why does Migrely keep everything on the phone?

A headache diary holds some of the most personal things a phone can know: when you were in pain, what you took and your cycle. Migrely keeps all of it on your phone and has no internet access, so it cannot send it to us or to anyone else. You can check that yourself, and see what Android's own backup and the files you choose to share do. See how other migraine diaries compare.

It is built for the moment a migraine starts: one tap to log, a dark screen, and the details later.

How does Migrely make money?

From Migrely Premium, and nothing else. Premium adds the doctor report and Insights. The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers; there is also a monthly plan and a one-time lifetime purchase. Google Play handles the payment, and we never see your card, name or email.

There are no ads, and no data is sold or shared: your entries never reach us, so there is nothing to sell.

What stays free?

Logging, your whole history, medications, reminders, backup and spreadsheet export, and every safety warning are free, for good.

What will Migrely never do?

  • Ask for internet access.
  • Ask you to make an account.
  • Show ads, or carry analytics or tracking code of its own.
  • Put a safety warning behind Premium.

Where do the medication limits come from?

Migrely is a diary, not a medical device. Its medication note uses the day counts for medication-overuse headache in the International Classification of Headache Disorders (ICHD-3): 15 or more days a month with simple painkillers, or 10 or more with triptans, combination painkillers or other medicines for attacks. Migrely counts your days over the last 30 and suggests talking to your doctor. It does not diagnose.

How can I contact you?

Help and questions: support@migrely.health. Privacy: privacy@migrely.health. Answers to common questions are on the help page.
