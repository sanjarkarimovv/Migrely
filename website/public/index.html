Everything a migraine diary should be

Log in seconds

One tap when a migraine starts, even in a dark room. Migrely keeps the time while you rest, and you can add the details later.

Your month as a calm sky

Every day is drawn from what you logged: clear, hazy, cloudy or a storm. Patterns show at a glance, with period and pain-free days marked.

Watch your medication days

A gentle note when painkillers or triptans reach the days a month that can make headaches more frequent, so you can talk to your doctor in time.

A report for your doctor

A clear PDF with a summary, a calendar and every migraine and medication, ready to share at your appointment.

See what your migraines share

Triggers often logged with your migraines, the days and hours they come, the days around your period and what helped.

Gentle on your eyes

Dark by default, light if you like, and always dark during a migraine. Large text, TalkBack and keyboards work throughout.

Private by design

  • Migrely has no internet access. Nothing you enter can leave your phone through the app.
  • No account to make, no tracking, no ads.
  • Your entries stay on your phone, and in Android's own backup, end-to-end encrypted on Android 9 and later.
  • Export a backup or a spreadsheet, or delete everything, whenever you like.
