Delete your data

Migrely keeps your entries only on your phone, so you delete them on your phone. We hold nothing about you that we could delete for you.

In the app

  1. Open Migrely, then Settings.
  2. Choose Delete all data at the end of the list.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm. Every entry, setting and copy Migrely keeps on the phone is removed, and the app starts fresh.

Android's own backup

Android's backup of Migrely is replaced the next time your phone backs up, usually within a day. You can also delete your phone's backup in your Google account's backup settings.

Premium

Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play: Profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions.
