# Migrely privacy policy

Effective date: TODO(user), the day the policy is published.

Migrely is a headache diary for Android, made by TODO(user: developer name) ("we"). This policy explains what
happens to the information you enter. In short: **Migrely has no internet access, and we never receive your entries.**

## What Migrely keeps, and where

Migrely keeps what you enter on your phone:
- migraines, with their times, pain levels, where it hurt, symptoms, possible triggers, how they affected you and your
  notes;
- medications and when you took them, and whether they helped;
- days you mark as period days or pain-free days;
- your settings, such as reminders, the theme and whether you track periods.

Migrely does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any account. It has no internet permission,
so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

## Android's own backup

Android may back up Migrely's entries and settings to your Google account, as it does for other apps, and restore them
on a new phone. On Android 9 and later, Migrely allows this backup only when it is end-to-end encrypted, which needs a
screen lock on your phone; without one, the entries move only to a new phone by direct transfer. Android 8 cannot
require that encryption, so there the backup works as it does for other apps. This backup is run by Google under
Google's privacy policy, and we cannot see it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

## Files you choose to share

You can export a backup file, a spreadsheet file or a PDF report for your doctor. Migrely creates the file, and you
choose where it goes, for example by email or to a cloud drive. What happens to it then depends on the service you
choose.

## Notifications

Reminders and the "Did it help?" question are notifications made on your phone. They need the notification permission,
which you can refuse or withdraw at any time.

## Deleting your information

- **Settings → Delete all data** removes every entry, setting and copy Migrely keeps on your phone.
- Uninstalling Migrely removes them too.
- Android's own backup of Migrely is replaced the next time your phone backs up, usually within a day. You can also
  delete it in your Google account's backup settings.

## Children

Migrely is meant for adults, and is not directed at children under 18.

## Consumer health data (Washington, Nevada, Connecticut and similar laws)

The information you enter in Migrely is consumer health data under laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act,
Nevada's SB 370 and Connecticut's health data rules. We do not collect, share or sell it: it stays on your phone, and
we have no way to receive it. Migrely uses no location data and no geofencing. Because we hold none of your health
data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete or share on request; you control it all in the app, as
described above. If you have a question about your rights, contact us at the address below.

## Changes to this policy
