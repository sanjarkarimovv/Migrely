# Health apps declaration

The answers for Play Console → App content → Health apps (BRD §11.2). Check the form's current list of features at
setup, and choose only what Migrely does.

## Features to declare

| Play's feature | Migrely | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Disease and condition management | Yes | a headache and migraine diary, with monthly statistics, patterns and a doctor's report |
| Medication and treatment management | Yes | logging medications and intakes, whether they helped, and the medication-overuse note |
| Period tracking | Yes | optional period-day marks, and migraines around the period |
| Sleep, stress, nutrition, fitness, mental health | No | |
| Clinical decision support, diagnosis, medical device functions | No | Migrely gives no diagnosis or treatment advice (BRD §11.1) |
| Health research | No | |
| Connects to health devices or Health Connect | No | |

## Statements to make

- **Not a medical device:** Migrely is a self-tracking diary, and it says so in the app, the listing and the PDF.
- **Regulatory status:** it is not cleared or approved by any health authority, and it makes no claim to be.
- **The medical text it shows:**
  - medication-overuse thresholds (ICHD-3);
  - the 72-hour attack note (status migrainosus);
  - warning signs that need urgent care.

  Each one advises talking to a doctor or calling the local emergency number, and never prescribes (BRD §11.9).
- **Health data:** stays on the phone, never transmitted, with no account (see `data-safety.md`).
