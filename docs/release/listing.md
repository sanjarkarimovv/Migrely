## Full description (≤ 4000)

```text
Migrely is a calm, private migraine diary. Log an attack in three taps, even in a dark room, and see your months as a
quiet night sky: a clear day, a hazy one, a storm.

LOG IN SECONDS
• "Migraine started" in one tap, in the app or by long-pressing its icon
• Pain level, where it hurts, symptoms, possible triggers and notes, all optional
• Attack mode stays dark and simple while you are in pain
• Add past migraines, edit any entry, and undo a deletion

SEE YOUR MONTHS
• A calendar where each day is a small sky, drawn only from what you logged
• Migraine days, attacks, medication days and pain-free days for every month
• Mark period days and pain-free days

KEEP AN EYE ON MEDICATION
• Log what you took, and later say whether it helped
• A gentle note when painkillers or triptans reach the days a month that can make headaches more frequent, so you can
  talk to your doctor about it

FOR YOUR DOCTOR
• A clear PDF report: a summary, a calendar and every migraine and medication, ready to share

UNDERSTAND YOUR PATTERNS
• Triggers often logged with your migraines, the days and hours they come, the days around your period, what helped,
  and the last 12 months

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Migrely has no internet access. There is no account, no tracking and no ads
• Your entries stay on your phone and in Android's own backup
• Export a backup or a spreadsheet, or delete everything, whenever you like

GENTLE ON YOUR EYES
• Dark by default, with a light theme, and always dark during a migraine
• Large text, TalkBack and keyboards work throughout

SAFETY FIRST
• The warning signs that need urgent care are always at hand in Settings

Migrely is a diary. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a
doctor about your headaches, and before you start, stop or change a medication. If a headache comes with any of the
warning signs, call your local emergency number.
```
